No implementation repair or empirical retry followed the finding.

## Exact interruption mechanism

`prepare()` calls the selective projection first, receiving all carrier,
receiver and defender coordinates in memory. It then registers the state and
its required edges, records the lifecycle state open, and emits individual edge
receipts. The receipt reducer currently defines unresolved edge exposure as
opened states minus registered states. Registration therefore clears uncertainty
before the per-edge receipts exist.

The synthetic projection spy returned two receiver points and incremented a
projected-edge counter to two. The injection raised immediately after the
unchanged registration transition. The preserved result reports
`edges_opened=0`, `unresolved_attempts=0` and
`states_with_unresolved_edge_exposure=0`. Its accepted failure snapshot has
zero preparation and evaluation completions. The lifecycle counts are no longer
inflated as in R3; the newly demonstrated defect is the gap in access history.

The second validation step reused the same retained synthetic journal and
terminal snapshot; it did not repeat projection. It wrote real temporary QC,
manifest, lifecycle and evidence files and invoked the production persisted
validator. Acceptance was observed. All detailed traces and the synthetic
journal remain ignored and hash-bound. The real failure package uses a separate
empty journal, so synthetic exposure is not confused with empirical exposure.
