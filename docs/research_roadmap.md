# Research roadmap

Status: active and conditional, aligned 2026-09-10. The numbered phases below
remain scientific gates rather than a claim that later governed sessions mapped
one-to-one onto the original sequence.

Completed work includes provenance and compatibility audits, a frozen
ball-carrier receiver-choice population, transparent M0/M1/M2 development
comparisons, and one protected ten-match evaluation. The major replicated result
is the M1 defensive-geometry receiver-ranking increment; M2 is a smaller,
metric-dependent refinement. Accessibility remains a proxy and suppression is
not supportable.

The current frontier is the unfinished Session 7 development-only construct and
model-behavior audit under [Phase 07a](protocols/phase_07a_human_review_withdrawal.md).
Human review was withdrawn before responses, and no continuation is authorized
automatically. Future optional work includes a simple attacking-option network
only if edge semantics become defensible, provider-independent software/API
integration, practitioner visualization and storytelling, separately validated
defensive structure, and submission/package hardening. Listing future work here
does not authorize it.

**Do not add PageRank, graph neural networks, community detection, or similar
machinery simply because players can be drawn as a graph. The project first
needs a defensible attacking edge.**

## Gates and failure paths

1. **Access and observation gate (0–1).** Verify permission and documentation
   before loading data. An absent ball signal, incompatible clocks, unknown
   direction, or unreliable identities may require a narrower construct. Record
   the unsupported use case; never fill a scientific unknown with a convenient
   default.
2. **Construct gate (2–3).** Separate pass selection, execution/completion,
   receiving accessibility, and progression value. If one edge bundles them,
   justify the combination and identify what validates each part. Reject an
   edge whose only justification is how it looks.
3. **Validation gate (4–5).** Freeze the primary candidate and comparisons before
   opening validation. Chosen passes reveal a selected subset of possibilities;
   unchosen receivers are not automatically failed passes. If suitable labels
   or support are missing, limit claims to what can actually be checked or stop.
4. **Attribution gate (6).** Multiple defenders can cover the same corridor.
   Redundancy, interactions, and unrealistic counterfactual positions can prevent
   identifiable individual contributions even when a team measure works.
5. **Complexity gate (7–9).** New graph summaries or utility models need a stated
   football reason and incremental validation. A validated simple result is a
   valid endpoint. Do not introduce sophistication to rescue a failed edge.

Pose is an optional branch after XY conventions are understood. With only two
expected games, test whether orientation adds useful information, whether
pose/XY synchronization is adequate, and whether an XY proxy can be evaluated
without circular labels. Reserve games/passages prospectively. If there is no
credible independent generalization assessment, keep conclusions local to pose
games or drop the branch.

## Prospective decisions and stopping

Each phase uses the protocol template in
[research_governance.md](research_governance.md). Exact thresholds, sample size,
minimum useful differences, tolerances, and statistical procedures remain
unset until the construct and available metadata justify them. They must be
recorded before relevant outcomes are viewed; unset criteria block evaluation.

Pause a phase when required access, variables, synchronization, sample support,
or reproducibility cannot be established. Reject or narrow a candidate when it
fails its predefined criteria, is unstable to reasonable measurement error, or
cannot beat the specified simpler baseline where incremental benefit is claimed.
Preserve negative, mixed, invalid, and stopped runs. A scientific stop does not
justify silently replacing metrics or reusing a spent holdout as untouched.

The supplied submission deadline is 18 December 2026. Work backward from that
date and protect time for clean-environment reproduction, package hardening,
visual communication, and honest claim review. The roadmap can end at a bounded
receiver-ranking contribution, measurement validation, or a documented failure
rather than promise a defensive-value model.
