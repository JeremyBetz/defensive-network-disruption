## Prospective numerical contract

The repair is a new internal adapter. Historical R9E/R9I/R9J implementation
paths remain unchanged. The production vector, strict/repeat piecewise
integrations, direct-Simpson check, joint-maximum comparison, historical
split-Simpson obligations, and certificate-aware piece behavior are composed
unchanged.

Before numerical comparison, an immutable structural-comparator authority is
constructed from the accepted canonical geometry. Its sole allowed boundaries
are domain endpoints, certified onset coordinates, accepted switch canonical
coordinates with their owner witnesses, and the two certified binary64
endpoints of each tie enclosure. The final partition must be finite, strictly
increasing, cover exactly `[0,1]`, and equal the exact sorted union of those
tagged coordinates. Missing, extra, corrupt, overlapping, uncovered, or
inconsistent authority fails closed. No boundary discovery occurs after a
numerical result.

The independent comparator evaluates the maximum field over that partition by
the existing bounded adaptive implementation, separate from the
certificate-aware `_integrate` route. It uses `epsabs=epsrel=1e-13`,
`limit=1000`, the fixed field bound `[0,1]`, and micro-residual eligibility
`width <= 1e-12/N` using the actual full positive-piece count. Ordinary point
contributions and residual intervals are stably summed and outward-rounded once
by the inherited bounded-adaptive implementation. A warning, exception, or
nonfinite result is blocking; the comparator has no certificate route.

The comparator returns an independently evaluated whole-integral interval. Its
interval distance from the unchanged strict piecewise interval must be at most
`1e-10`. The compatibility stage remains `onset_adaptive` and the compatibility
gate token remains `piecewise_unsplit`; the report must state that their R9K
semantics are structure-aware. This naming preserves the existing publication
schema and does not preserve the defective onset-only partition choice.
