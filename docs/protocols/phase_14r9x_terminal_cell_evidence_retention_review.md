## Purpose and boundaries

R9X is a metadata-only evidence-retention review. It preserves R9V as
**NF / PA / readiness 3** and records the subsequent uncommitted R9W read-only
stop as **TE / NF / readiness 4**. R9W found that the 81 retained cell records
contain only ordinal, depth, pair status and maximum status, leaving the one
unresolved terminal cell without directly retained bounds, mathematical
function authority or numerical enclosures.

R9X accesses zero empirical states and zero empirical edges. It may inspect
tracked source and the schemas, filenames and hashes of retained private
records. It must not read `selected_edge.json` contents, prepared or provider
data, raw geometry, field values, owner certification, localization, scientific
summaries, targets, models, outcomes or shares. It performs no empirical
numerical computation, acquisition, repair or retry.

## Evidence-loss trace

The review traces each datum from exact inputs through `FieldAuthority`,
`PairAuthority`, `Cell`, the `reference()` refinement loop, its sink and the
private index. It must locate the exact source and serialization boundary for
cell bounds, function coefficients, pair and competitor authority, branch
state, value/difference/derivative enclosures and refinement provenance.

The frozen planning finding is that `Cell.left`, `Cell.right`, the field and
pair authorities, and temporary enclosures existed in memory. The sink wrote
only ordinal, depth, `pair_status` and `maximum_status`. Exact leaf bounds are
prospectively derivable from the retained outer interval and the complete
ordered leaf-depth partition, but R9W had no frozen derivation contract and
correctly refused reconstruction. Coefficient authority is absent from the cell
records.

## Minimum terminal-cell authority

R9X defines an immutable, canonical, versioned `TerminalCellAuthority`. It
contains exact rational cell bounds; cell ordinal and depth; candidate and
formula authority; exact constant-width coefficient records (`q`, `dot`,
`cross2`) for the tied pair and every unique competitor needed for global
maximality; pair and competitor references by coefficient hash; R9V
private-index, boundary-capture, ordered-partition, selected-edge and
implementation hashes; and a provenance hash over the complete payload.

The fixed constant-width formula derives `rho`, `alpha`, `beta2`, branch/onset
state, field/difference/derivative bounds and refinement state. Previous
enclosures, parent contents, production owner decisions and duplicated derived
coefficients are not retained. Aliases, identities, coordinates, tracking rows
and provider records are prohibited.

Exact fractions use canonical signed decimal numerators and positive decimal
denominators in lowest terms. Records are strictly field-allowlisted, finite,
deterministically ordered and content hashed. The synthetic loader accepts only
this object and has no geometry or empirical route.

## Synthetic sufficiency and acquisition boundary

Provider-free fixtures create an authority from known functions, discard all
source geometry, reload only canonical serialized evidence and independently
recover pair-maximal, third-defender-dominant, dominance-switch, interior-
equality and unresolved outcomes. Negative controls cover changed coefficients
or bounds, missing competitors or fields, wrong authority hashes, noncanonical
fractions, nonfinite values and unsupported versions.

A future acquisition, if separately governed, may validate the existing R9V
private index, boundary capture, all 81 ordered leaf records and selected-edge
hash; read only the retained R9V selected-edge copy; derive coefficient
authorities without evaluating fields; derive the unresolved leaf bounds under
the frozen ordered-depth partition contract; persist one authority object; and
stop before refinement, interpretation or repair. This counts one previously
exposed state and one previously exposed edge reopened, with no new population
exposure. R9X does not perform it.
