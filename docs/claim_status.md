Allowed statuses: UNTESTED, IN PROGRESS, SUPPORTED WITHIN SCOPE, MIXED,
NOT SUPPORTED, INVALID, or WITHDRAWN. “Supported” always names the tested scope
and is not universal proof. Invalid means an evaluation cannot answer its
question; it is distinct from an informative negative result. Withdrawal does
not erase the original hypothesis or its evidence.

For every change, append the date, claim ID, previous/new status, protocol and
run references, evidence, scope, uncertainty, deviations, and next decision. Do
not replace history with the most favorable result. Apply the escalation rules
in [research governance](research_governance.md).
