NOT SUPPORTED, INVALID, or WITHDRAWN. “Supported” must always specify the tested
scope and is not universal proof. Invalid means an evaluation cannot answer its
question; it is distinct from an informative negative result. Withdrawal does
not erase the original hypothesis or its evidence.
